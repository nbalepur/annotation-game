User Study Incentive Structure

Eligibility Criteria

Participants must answer at least 10 questions to be eligible for incentives.

For every five questions answered, you will be awarded five dollars. There are a maximum of 300 questions that can be answered (150 in each type).

Rewards Per Round
  • Top 3 in Accuracy: Each receives a $50 gift card.
  • Top 3 in Speed: Each receives a $50 gift card.
  • We will take the Top 3 winners in both question types (math and trivia). You are eligible to win in both categories!
University Coursework

If you were recruited to this study from a University course in exchange for extra credit, your Professor will later set up an assignment on your course website where you can submit the email you used to complete the study. Once we verify you completed the study, your professor will distribute your extra credit accordingly. If you decide to complete our study and do not fill out the assignment in your class, you are still eligible for the monetary compensation above.

Please note that you are only eligible for either monetary compensation or coursework credit, not both.

If you have any questions, please reach out to the email created for this project. If you don't receive a response within a day or your issue is urgent, please reach out to Nishant Balepur, one of the researchers leading this project, via email.
